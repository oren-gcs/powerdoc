# Field-to-office thesis

## Correction from the session

**Invoices are one example. Routing is the product.**

Do not let the pitch collapse into “DocFlow is an invoice OCR app.” The desk moves **any** operational document: site checklists, vendor onboarding, class summaries, AP packets, safety forms.

## Thesis in one breath

Field workers capture evidence (photo, form, WhatsApp, file) → DocFlow turns it into a **desk object** with class, fields, and a **route** → office systems (accounting, Ram-Control views, CRM/BI) consume the result.

## Bridges called out

| Bridge | Session note |
|---|---|
| **WhatsApp** | Natural Israeli field channel — bridge is desired; not the demo surface for tomorrow |
| **Offline PWA** | Gap for field sites with bad connectivity — roadmap, not a claim |
| Public fill + sign | Exists in powerdoc today — good for “office receives a completed form” |

## Pitch framing

1. Open with **movement**: documents should move from field to office.
2. Show **one** invoice path as the concrete wedge.
3. Zoom out: same pipe for site / vendor / education forms.
4. Close ask: CPA intros for the health-check wedge.
