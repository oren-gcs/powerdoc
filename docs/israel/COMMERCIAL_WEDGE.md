# Commercial wedge — בדיקת בריאות חשבוניות

## Offer

- **Name:** בדיקת בריאות חשבוניות (invoice health check)
- **Price band (session):** **₪8–15k** one-shot engagement
- **Motion:** client gives a **12-month export**; you return an **exposure report in ₪**. Zero install, zero risk — second meeting opens with *their* number.
- **Channel:** **CPA / accountant** introductions — one firm brings dozens of clients; they are already the gatekeeper. Not a cold enterprise SaaS close on day one.

| A paid diagnostic that proves the desk | Multi-year ERP replacement |
| CPA-trusted language | “AI that replaces bookkeepers” |
